Maintenance & Road Tax

In many European jurisdictions, EVs benefit from zero road tax (such as Portuguese IUC) and lower brake/oil service costs (~€150-€300/yr extra savings).

Real-World Consumption

Winter temperature conditions and motorway driving can raise EV consumption up to 16–18 kWh/100km, while heavy urban stop-and-go favors EV efficiency.

Solar PV Synergy

Charging from home rooftop photovoltaic generation can drop electricity costs down to €0.04 - €0.06/kWh, shortening the break-even horizon by over 40%.
